About — cell A2

The short version

I'm a Computer Science student at Benha National University, building a freelance data analytics practice alongside my degree. My work starts where most dashboards fall apart: the cleaning stage — fixing inconsistent types, dropping what can't be trusted, and deciding what actually belongs in the analysis.

From there I move into Python and SQL to explore the data, and into Power BI or Excel to turn it into something a client or stakeholder can read in thirty seconds and act on. I've applied that process to retail, streaming, e-commerce, and HR datasets — each one is in the projects below.

Outside of coursework, I'm actively growing my presence on Khamsat and Mostaql, working directly with clients on real analytics briefs.
